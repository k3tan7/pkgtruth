Decoding Release Decisions

Reference

Every investigation evaluates against your rules and assigns one of three transparent verdicts:

ALLOW

Allowed under configured rules

All enabled checks completed without triggering blocking or review conditions. Reminder: No static analysis can guarantee complete safety or absence of zero-days.

REVIEW

Needs human review

Differences, unverified source correspondence, or coverage gaps were identified. Review the findings and changed files before approving installation.

BLOCK

Blocked by configured rules

A blocking condition was triggered. Note: corrupt wheel RECORD manifests or invalid internal checksums always trigger an immediate BLOCK regardless of toggles.

Deterministic integrity: Local AI models can explain evidence, but they can NEVER alter, override, or relax your deterministic policy decisions.
